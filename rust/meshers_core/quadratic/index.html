Quadratic (10-node) tetrahedra whose wall edges follow the exact implicit surface.

Mid-edge nodes of implicit-wall edges are Newton-projected onto the wall, holding any box coordinate the edge lies in, so the geometric error of the boundary drops from O(h^2) to O(h^3). Interior and box-plane mid-edge nodes stay at midpoints. Each element’s Jacobian determinant is sampled at its nodes, face and cell centres and interior points; corrections of failing elements are repeatedly halved, together with their periodic copies. Unresolved edges are straightened after eight reductions. A whole-cell determinant bound also checks every curved proposal.
