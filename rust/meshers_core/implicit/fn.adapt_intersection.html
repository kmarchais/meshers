Adapt a conforming nonperiodic Tet4 seed using the same physical fields. Mask bits identify the supplied fields. The seed must pass a separate sampled seed_tolerance; final quality and geometry use options without relaxation. The caller owns the seed’s topology and resolution of disconnected features.
