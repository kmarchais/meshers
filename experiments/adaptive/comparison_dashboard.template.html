Method, limits and evidence
  • Admission requires boundary distance ≤0.002, absolute relative volume error ≤1%, corner q ≥0.1, positive sampled curved Jacobians, coherent facets and quadratic associations, required periodic nodes/caps, and at most 250,000 nodes and tets including actual intermediate meshes.
  • Boundary distance is a finite two-sided sampled observation. It is not a certified Hausdorff bound. Volume-reference scramble spread is empirical, not a confidence interval.
  • Both arms use the same fixed material field and samples. Actual workflows start from genuine Microgen seeds. The default and modified recipes remain separate.
  • Tet10 uses curved Meshers boundaries and explicitly straight-elevated actual elements. Equal polynomial order does not imply equal geometry representation. Counts include nodes so Tet4 and Tet10 costs remain visible.
  • Times are fresh M4 generation medians with one numerical thread. They include arm-specific preparation, extraction, MMG retries, explicit repair/elevation and conversion. They exclude common imports, fixture loading, archival I/O and independent audits. The experimental cap-fitting time includes its Python detection/fitting pass and its import. They are not complete FEA workflow times.
  • Paired speedups use their own five interleaved samples, not pooled actual medians. Historical native timings use the preserved baseline binary; follow-up timings use the repaired binary. Exact regeneration checks preserve seven prior curved outputs. These are distinct measured builds.
  • The Schwarz D follow-up corrects material-trace matching without moving geometry or changing tolerances. Its old all-plane rejections remain available in the history filter.
  • The linear grades have unequal opposite-z material traces. Both arms use bounded nonperiodic meshing. The density ramp is calibrated from 27% to 45% cross-sectional material area fraction. The separate thickness case varies a level-set parameter.
  • “Fewer elements, quality and geometry no worse” checks every recorded quality quantile, both geometry errors and node count. Volume differences below the reference spread remain unresolved. Passing common admission gates alone does not mean equal quality.
  • Missing or rejected comparators never generate speedup ratios. A recipe improvement does not establish universal geometry coverage. Full matched-stiffness superiority remains unproven.
