Periodicity and quality¶

Request periodicity explicitly¶

mesh = meshers.generate("gyroid", band=(-0.5, 0.5), cells=24, periodic=(True, True, True))

The field must match under translations across each requested pair of box faces. Linear grading across an axis generally breaks that periodicity. Without a constraint, matching boundary meshes are not guaranteed, even for a periodic field.

periodic_pairs contains matching node IDs for each axis. VTKHDF stores PeriodicMasterId and physical PeriodicShift point arrays. Applications that transform a mesh must transform its periodic shift vectors consistently.

Inspect the result¶

Measure Interpretation
Positive tetrahedron volume Necessary for a valid element orientation
MMG quality 1 for a regular tetrahedron; approaching 0 indicates degeneracy
Sampled boundary error Estimated geometric deviation in physical units
Volume distribution Very small elements can increase FEM conditioning and timestep costs

VTKHDF includes integrated Volume. Tet4 exports MMGQuality; Tet10 exports CornerVolume and CornerMMGQuality for its linear parent. The diagnostic sampled_surface_error also describes the linear parent, not a distance audit of the final curved faces. Check curved geometry separately when needed. Quality uses the MMG formula in NumPy; it does not call mmgpy. Boundary tags are 0 for implicit faces, then 1 and 2 for x-/x+, 3 and 4 for y-/y+, and 5 and 6 for z-/z+.

Increase resolution and check solution convergence for the intended FEM model. Minimum quality alone does not establish simulation accuracy. Small components can be missed by sampling, and the geometry check is not a global proof.

Invalid options raise ValueError; unsuccessful geometry/topology checks raise MeshingError. A CancellationToken allows cooperative cancellation, including from another Python thread. Callback exceptions are preserved.

Reject missing or disconnected material¶

When the intended topology is known, pass expected_components to meshers.generate. For example, expected_components=2 requires two groups of tetrahedra connected across full triangular faces. If a coarse lattice misses a small island beside a larger solid, the call raises MeshingError even though the larger solid produced valid elements. An unexpected split in a thin ligament also changes the count. Counting uses the cut box before identifying periodic images. Vertex and edge contacts do not join components.

The count is opt-in. It cannot identify which island survived or detect a missing feature if another defect preserves the count. A sampled-empty mesh has zero components. For order=2 its connectivity has shapes (0, 10) for tetrahedra and (0, 6) for surface triangles.

Curved element orientation¶

Tet10 elevation and optional curved fitting check the Jacobian determinant throughout each proposed curved element. Its matrix varies affinely over the reference tetrahedron. Products of its corner rows bound the determinant between the sample points. The mesher bisects inconclusive regions. A fold or an unresolved bound causes elevation to reduce the edge correction, or fitting to reject the proposed step. The reported minimum_jacobian_ratio is still the ratio measured at the sampling points.

Require a minimum element quality¶

mesh = meshers.generate(
    "gyroid",
    band=(-0.5, 0.5),
    cells=24,
    periodic=(True, True, True),
    minimum_quality=0.1,
)

minimum_quality is a required lower bound on the MMG tetrahedron-quality metric, in [0, 1]. The default 0 keeps the earlier geometry-only acceptance policy. A nonempty mesh below the requested bound raises MeshingError and returns no partial mesh. Empty geometries remain valid empty results. The same option is available as implicit::Options::minimum_quality in Rust.

The example value 0.1 is an explicit acceptance choice, not a universal FEM criterion. This option does not automatically remesh, change the field, or certify solver accuracy. Appropriate shape quality, element sizes, conditioning and solution convergence depend on the problem.

Diagnostics include minimum_mmg_quality, elements_below_quality_01 (a count), and reverted_snap_vertices. Positive volumes and finite positive quality are checked regardless of the requested floor.

Safe snapping and boundary checks¶

Snapping is retained where it passes the boundary checks. If snapped lattice vertices introduce non-manifold boundary edges or vertex links, Meshers rolls back those snaps and recomputes the cuts. Periodic copies are reverted together. This repair is limited to eight clipping attempts; unresolved topology still raises an error. It does not shift the cell phase, delete connected components, or relax the boundary-error limit.

Both edge incidence and vertex links are checked before optimization and on the finished mesh. Two shells touching at a single vertex are rejected even when every edge has exactly two incident triangles. These combinatorial checks do not prove that the mesh is free of geometric self-intersections or unresolved features.
