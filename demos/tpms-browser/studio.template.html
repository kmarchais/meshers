Runs locally in WebAssembly. No geometry is uploaded. Presets are experimental settings, not certification for printing or analysis.

Printing uses exact intersections without triangle polishing. Deviation is a first-order distance estimate at triangle centroids, not a maximum error bound. Closure checks edge incidence and orientation, not self-intersections. FEA targets minimum MMG tetrahedron quality ≥ 0.1 and sampled boundary error ≤ 1% of a unit cell. A missed target is reported, not hidden. Cutting the view does not modify exported meshes.
